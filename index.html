About me

I started out designing and teaching structured learning programs in the IDF, then led live cyber training at Cyber School. Most recently I built enablement for global sales teams and partners at Radware.

I hold a B.A. in Instructional Technologies from Holon Institute of Technology, where I also teach Figma and Articulate Storyline. I use AI tools to move faster on content, and I keep the learner’s real job at the centre of every design.
